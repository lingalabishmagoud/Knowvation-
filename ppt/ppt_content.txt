SLIDE 2: Project Overview
Title: What is Knowvation?
Bullet Points:
- A full-stack AI-driven hiring intelligence web application.
- Automates the extraction and analysis of job market trends.
- Scrapes raw job data and uses Google Gemini AI for structured NLP analysis.
- Provides recruiters with a dynamic 3D-inspired analytics dashboard.

SLIDE 3: Problem Statement
Title: The Problem We Are Solving
Bullet Points:
- Manual collection of job market data is slow and inefficient.
- Hard to track competitor hiring trends in real-time.
- Identifying highly demanded skills requires reading hundreds of descriptions.
- Solution: An automated scraper + AI analysis pipeline to generate instant insights.

Title: Technologies Used

- Backend: Python, FastAPI
- Web Scraping: Playwright
- AI Engine: Google Gemini API
- Database & Auth: Appwrite
